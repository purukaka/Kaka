AI Tips & Prompts

Prompts I actually use.

No fluff — six prompts pulled straight from film, business, and content work. Copy one and edit the brackets.

Content Creation

Turn One Idea Into a Week of Content

I create content about [topic]. Take this single idea — [describe your idea] — and break it into 5 distinct pieces for [platform], each covering a different angle (a hook, a myth-bust, a how-to, a personal story, a listicle). For each, give me a headline and a 2-sentence outline.
Filmmaking

Short Film Treatment Builder

I have a short film idea: [describe premise in 1-2 sentences]. Help me build a one-page treatment: logline, 3-act beat structure (setup / turn / resolution), the central visual motif, and one scene I should shoot first as a proof-of-concept.
Online Business

Business Idea Stress-Test

Here's a business idea: [describe it]. Act as a skeptical but constructive advisor. Identify the 3 biggest assumptions this idea depends on, the cheapest way to test each one within a week, and the single most likely reason this fails.
AI / Productivity

AI as a Thinking Partner

I'm deciding between [option A] and [option B] for [context]. Don't tell me which to pick. Instead, ask me the 5 questions I should be able to answer before I can decide well — one at a time.
Content Creation

One Piece, Five Platforms

Here's a long-form piece I wrote: [paste it]. Repurpose it into: a 3-line Instagram caption, a 6-tweet thread, a 60-second video script, and a one-paragraph newsletter blurb — keep my voice, don't add generic hype language.
Filmmaking

Scene Punch-Up

Here's a scene from my script: [paste scene]. Keep the plot exactly the same, but punch up the dialogue to feel more natural and specific to each character. Flag any line that's doing pure exposition so I can see it.
