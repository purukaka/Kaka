About

A builder who tells stories, and a storyteller who builds.

I started Okay Pasal in 2018 because I wanted to understand commerce from the inside — not as theory, but as a business I had to run. Since then, I've trained in film direction at Kantipur Film Academy and worked on photography and visual production at Percept Media Production. The through-line is the same in every case: understand the audience, shape the story, ship the work.
